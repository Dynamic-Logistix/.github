## Welcome

This is the internal developer portal where DLX builds the software that moves freight. You'll find the TMS work, the integration hub, the MCP servers that connect our data to AI tools, the internal apps the team uses every day, and the plumbing that keeps it all running.

## What lives here

Repos are grouped by what they do:

- **XTMS and integrations**
- **Data and analytics**
- **AI and MCP servers**
- **Internal apps**
- **Infrastructure and tooling**

## How we work

- **Keep it simple.** Build the smallest thing that solves the problem.
- **Think before you code.** Say what you're assuming, and ask when you're unsure.
- **Keep dependencies lean.** Every new library is a commitment.
- **Never commit secrets.** Use Azure Key Vault or your local environment, not the repo.

## Our values

- **Positive Mental Attitude.** Realistic, results-driven, confident without arrogance.
- **Collaborative Approach.** Humble team players, always willing to learn.
- **Do the Right Thing.** Truthful with teammates, customers, and partners.
- **Get S#!t Done.** Gritty, tenacious, consistent. No excuses.
