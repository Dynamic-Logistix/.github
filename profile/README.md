## Welcome

This is the home of DLX Solutions, the team that builds the software that moves freight. You'll find the TMS work, the integration hub, the MCP servers that connect our data to AI tools, the internal apps the team uses every day, and the plumbing that keeps it all running.

## What lives here

Repos are grouped by what they do:

- **XTMS and integrations**
- **Data and analytics**
- **AI and MCP servers**
- **Internal apps**
- **Infrastructure and tooling**

## How we work

- **Keep it simple.** Build the smallest thing that solves the problem.
- **Think before you code.** Say what you're assuming, and ask when you're unsure.
- **Keep dependencies lean.** Every new library is a commitment.
- **Never commit secrets.** Credentials stay out of the repo.

## Our values

- **We bring a positive mental attitude every day.** We are realistic, results-driven, and confident.
- **We have a collaborative approach.** We are humble team players that are willing to learn.
- **We do the right thing.** We are truthful in our interactions with teammates, customers, and partners.
- **We get sh\*t done.** We are gritty, tenacious, and consistent.
